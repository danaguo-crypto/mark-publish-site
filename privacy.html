Mark publish — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

"Mark publish" is an internal tool used by Dana Guo Real Estate Associates (Dana Team, Long Island, New York) to publish the team's own videos to the team's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no outside users.

What we access

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool uploads our own videos and sets their title, description, thumbnail, playlist and publish time, and reads the status and view counts of those videos. It uses the YouTube API Services.

What we store

Only the IDs, links and view counts of our own videos, and the OAuth token needed to upload them. The token is stored on our own computer/server with owner-only access. We do not collect, sell or share any data about other YouTube users.

Google and YouTube policies

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
